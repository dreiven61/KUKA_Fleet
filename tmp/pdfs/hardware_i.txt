2 Product description 
2.1 Overview 
 
Fig. 2-1: KMP 1500I 
Description 
The vehicle is an automated guided vehicle (AGV), supported by QR code and 
SLAM navigation. 
The vehicle can transport goods and lift objects automatically, accurately and 
quickly. 
The vehicle can go straight, reverse, rotate, make U-turns, arc across, and lift or 
lower rack. 
The vehicle has the following three operating modes: 
• AUTO 
(>>>> AUTO "Auto" Page 65) 
• Debug 
(>>>>Debug "Debug" Page 65) 
• Manual 
(>>>>Manual "Manual" Page 65) 
The vehicle is powered by electricity. 
The vehicle has the following features: 
1. Load capacity of up to 1500 kg. 
2. Effective obstacle detection via lidar at the front, single-point TOF, and 3D 
binocular camera at the front and rear of the vehicle. 
3. Continuous working for 8 hours on full charge under standard conditions. 
4. Optional for 5G.

provided again after particular incidents or technical modifications. 
Personnel include the user and operator of the system. 
 
System integrator 
The vehicle must be safely integrated into a complete 
system by the system integrator. The system integrator 
is responsible for the following tasks: 
◼ Installing the vehicle 
◼ Connecting the vehicle 
◼ Performing the risk assessment for the application (e.g. Transporting, lifting 
and transferring the load, load lifting attachment) 
◼ Performing the risk assessment 
– Protecting the travel routes against unauthorized access 
– Securing the transport load against toppling and falling given the 
specified values for acceleration and deceleration 
– Providing escape routes in the complete system for the 
maintenance personnel (in accordance with the standard for 
automated guided vehicle systems valid in the user's country)  
– Labeling crush hazard points and warning of unintentional vehicle 
motion in the loading and unloading station 
◼ Implementing the required safety functions and safeguards 
◼ Providing escape routes and labeling danger zones in accordance with the 
applicable national standards for automated guided vehicle systems 
◼ Issuing the declaration of conformity 
◼ Creating the operating instructions for the complete system 
◼ Ensuring that, despite the specified positional accuracy of the vehicle, the 
load Lifting tackles always engage cleanly with the transport load and that 
this never slips on the vehicle 
◼ Ensuring that the center of gravity of the intended transport loads lies within 
the payload diagram

Work on the electrical and mechanical equipment of the vehicle may only

5 Planning 
5.1 Site requirements 
5.1.1 Road undulation 
When the vehicle is moving on a path within the permissible undulation range, it 
can be controlled in a nominal speed. The permissible undulation range is the 
difference between the maximal and minimal height in reference range.  
Within the area of 1 m² , the maximum undulation must be less than or 
equal to 6 mm. 
 
5.1.2 Road slope 
Description 
The road slope is the maximum ratio of horizontal difference to length for roads 
over 100 mm. It can be controlled at rated speed when the vehicle is operated 
within the maximum road slope. 
At stations where precise positioning of vehicles is required, the maximum road 
slope must be less than 1% (inclusive). 
In addition, the maximum road slope must be no greater than 5%. 
5.1.3 Step height 
Description 
The step height is the maximum height difference in a path over 100 mm. It can 
be controlled at the rated speed when the vehicle is operated on the road within 
the maximum step height. 
Do not park the vehicle on the steps. The maximum step height must be less 
than 5 mm (inclusive). 
 
5.1.4 Width range 
Description 
It can be controlled at the rated speed when the vehicle is operated on a path 
within the maximum width range. Do not park the vehicle on the groove.  
The maximum width range must be less than 35 mm (inclusive). When the width 
range exceeds the allowable value, it must conform to the requirements of step 
height. 
5.1.5 Ground friction coefficient 
Description 
The caster wheels are made of PU materials and the floor friction coefficient 
must be greater than 0.5. 
The floor friction coefficient has important influence on safe braking distance 
and positioning accuracy. Dirty floors, standing water, and detergents can also 
affect the operation of vehicles.

of vehicles.

11 Decommissioning, storage and disposal 
11.1 Decommissioning 
 
◼ The power switch of the vehicle is in the OFF position and secured to 
prevent unauthorized persons from switching it on again.  
◼ The vehicle has been disconnected from the drive battery. 
11.2 Storage 
If the system is to be put into long-term storage, the following points must be 
observed: 
◼ The place of storage must be as dry and dust-free as possible.  
◼ Avoid temperature fluctuations. 
◼ Avoid wind and drafts. 
◼ Avoid condensation. 
◼ Use appropriate coverings that cannot detach themselves and which 
can withstand the expected environmental conditions. 
◼ Do not leave any loose parts on the vehicle, especially ones that might 
knock against other parts. 
◼ Do not leave the system exposed to direct sunlight while in storage.  
◼ Observe and comply with the permissible temperature ranges for 
storage. 
◼ Select a storage location in which the packaging film cannot be 
damaged. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Risk of fire due to exhaustive discharge.  
If the drive batteries are stored without charging, exhaustive discharge will 
occur. Exhaustive discharge may lead to a short circuit and cause the fire. 
 • Recharge drive batteries every three months to compensate for self-
discharge. 
NOTICE 
Instructions and information about storing the drive battery are contained in 
the manufacturer documentation. 
Risk of explosion, fire and escape of hazardous gases. 
 If the defective or exhaustively discharged drive batteries are not correctly 
disposed of, the explosion, fire and hazardous gases may occur. 
 • Dispose of the defective drive batteries according to the related laws and 
regulations

Cleaning the vehicle 
1. No dirt may remain on or in the vehicle. (>>>>9.2.1"Cleaning" Page 
79) 
2.  Inspect the vehicle, both internally and externally. 
3.  Charge the drive battery every 3 months. 
4.  Turn the main switch to OFF position and secure it with a padlock to 
prevent unauthorized actuation.   
5. Remove any foreign bodies. 
6.  Remove any corrosion expertly. 
7.  Attach all housing to the vehicle. 
8.  Seal off electrical connections with suitable covers. 
9.  Immobilize the vehicle with chocks 
10. Cover the vehicle with weather- and tear-resistant plastic sheeting and 
seal it at the base frame against dust. If necessary, add a desiccant 
beneath the sheeting. 
11. When putting back into operation: charge the drive battery.
