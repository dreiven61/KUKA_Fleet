2 Product description
2.1 Overview
Fig. 2-1: KMP 1500P series
KMP 1500P series AMR can be used to transport materials from one sta-
tion to another in an industrial setting in a safe, efficient, and flexible man-
ner.
The AMR is a differential vehicle. It can, therefore, move forward and
backward. It is also equipped with a lifting stroke of 60 mm.
The AMR has the following features:
• Electrically driven
• Payload capacity of up to 1500 kg
• Safety features include 360-degree coverage using two laser scan-
ners, 3D cameras for obstacle detection, and E-stop bumper
• Up to ten hours of motion on a single battery charge
• Optional 5G support
• Optional inductive charging
Within the KMP 1500P series, there are:
• KMP 1500P-EU-D diffDrive
• KMP 1500P-EU-D-5G diffDrive
• KMP 1500P-EU-DIC diffDrive
• KMP 1500P-EU-DIC-5G diffDrive
Operating modes
The AMR can be operated in the following modes:
1. Auto mode
2. Maintenance mode
• Maintenance low velocity (T1)
• Maintenance high velocity (T2)
3. Brake release mode ( >>> 8.4 "Changing operating modes" Page 111 )

2.2.3 Lifting system
The lifting system is based on multi-link lift-guidance mechanism. Using
the lifting system, a transport load can be raised and set down at the des-
tination.
WARNING
It must be ensured that the AMR is not overloaded. The lifting system
does not detect an overload and is thus not automatically switched off.
This can result in damage to the AMR and danger to the persons in the
vicinity of the AMR.

2.2.8.1 Laser scanners
There are two laser scanners on the AMR. Each laser scanner can detect
obstacles at the height of 170 mm above the ground and located within
270° in front of the laser scanner.
The laser scanners monitor the area around the AMR. This monitored
area is the protective field. If the protective field is violated, the AMR
stops. The protective field can have a maximum length of 3 m.
Fig. 2-23: Laser scanners
1 Laser scanner, Back-right
2 Laser scanner, Front-left
In operating mode AUTO, the default size of the protective field increases
gradually with the travel speed. Refer to ( >>> 3.4.2 "Personnel detection
system" Page 40 ) for details.
Error acknowledgement
Automatic acknowledgement of the error state is carried out in the follow-
ing cases:
• As soon as the protective field becomes free.
• If the protective field length is reduced due to a decrease in speed
and thus obstacles are no longer within the protective field.
The laser scanner conforms to laser protection class 1. Additional meas-
ures for shielding the laser radiation are not required (eye-safe).

The following safety measures must be observed:
• Protect the laser scanner against dirt and damage.
• Avoid strong electrical fields. These can be caused, for example, by
welding cables or induction loops in the vicinity, but also by mobile tel-
ephones operated nearby.
• Keep the area protected free from smoke, vapor, steam and other air
contamination. The functioning of the laser scanner may otherwise be
adversely affected and malfunctions may occur.
• Avoid highly reflective objects in the scan plane of the laser scanner.
Examples: Reflectors can influence the measurement results of the la-
ser scanner. Reflective objects may conceal part of the surface to be
monitored.
• Protect the laser scanner from the glare of incident sunlight. Do not
position stroboscopic and fluorescent lamps or other strong sources of
light directly at the scan level since these can influence the laser
scanner under certain circumstances.
WARNING
When the operator controls the AMR in T1/T2 mode with the personnel
detection system deactivated, the operator bears sole responsibility for
the safety of the AMR.
The safety measures must be observed when operating the laser scan-
ner.
(>>> 3.6.2 "Laser devices" Page 47 )
WARNING
Following modification of the monitoring range, e.g. after adaptation to a

3.4.3 Reaction times of the safety functions
The reaction time describes the time between the following events:
• Time t 0 at which the event occurs that is to trigger a safety reaction,
e.g. entering a danger zone or setting an EMERGENCY STOP input
• Time t 1 at which the safety reaction is initiated, e.g. stop reaction is in-
itiated or an output has been deactivated
The reaction time thus contains fault detection times and delays before in-
itiation of the safety reaction. The worst-case reaction time in the case of
a single fault considers the presence of an individual fault and is thus
greater than the reaction time typically expected for the safety function.
The reaction time does not include the time between initiation of a stop
reaction and the AMR coming to a standstill (braking time = t 2 – t 1).

5.6.1 Key points of layout

more than 25 meters. The number of APs must be reasonable, other-

coverage of 15m is recommended.

• Deenergize system components and secure them against being
switched on again.
• After the machine and battery charging system have been switched
off: wait 2 minutes.
• Verify isolation from voltage at all poles.
Failure to take this into consideration may result in severe injuries or
even death.

12 Decommissioning, storage and disposal
12.1 Decommissioning
Precondition
• The AMR is switched off and secured.
• The drive battery is removed from the AMR.
12.2 Storage
Precondition
If the system is to be put into long-term storage, the following points must
be observed:
• The place of storage must be as dry and dust-free as possible.
• Avoid temperature fluctuations.
• Avoid wind and drafts.
• Avoid condensation.
• Use appropriate coverings that cannot detach themselves and which
can withstand the expected environmental conditions.
• Do not stack vehicles on top of each other.
• Do not leave any loose parts on the vehicle, especially ones that
might knock against other parts.
• Do not leave the system exposed to direct sunlight while in storage.
• Observe and comply with the permissible temperature ranges for stor-
age.
• Select a storage location in which the packaging film cannot be dam-
aged.
Procedure
1. Clean the AMR. No dirt may remain on or in the AMR.
2. Inspect the AMR, both internally and externally.
3. Remove the drive battery.
The drive batteries and the fuses of the drive batteries may only be
removed and installed by qualified specialist personnel.
4. Remove any foreign objects.
5. Remove any corrosion.
6. Screw all covers onto the AMR.
